# CHL Front Identification Archive - 2026-05-20

This archive freezes the CHL-front work so the active product path can return to thermal fronts.

## What Was Tried

- BOA/local-histogram style CHL front extraction on log chlorophyll.
- Multi-scale log-gradient ridge extraction for CHL.
- Log-Laplacian zero-crossing extraction for CHL.
- SIED/Woo-style SST comparison as a control.
- Mesoscale Gaussian mutual-overlap CHL approach from the CTO workspace.

## Result

The CHL methods were not production-ready. The gradient/ridge and bilateral/Sobel families produced hundreds of fragmented fronts and were sensitive to cloud/NaN edges. The mesoscale Gaussian mutual-overlap method was physically cleaner, but too conservative on the sampled VIIRS frame.

The better product direction is to focus back on thermal fronts first, where the signal is more stable and already has a usable SST-front data product.

## Decision

Do not promote CHL-front detection to the user-facing viewer yet. Use thermal fronts as the production front layer and keep CHL-front work as research evidence until we have a better algorithm or training data.
